Montana · active corridor

Specialized mobile RV repair serving Billings, MT

Billings is Montana’s largest city and the main I-90 waypoint toward Yellowstone. Dealership-trained, Victron Professional Certified Installer mobile service comes to your campsite, driveway, or storage yard - not a handyman drop-in.

Serving RVers around Billings

Whether you’re staged near the Yellowstone River off I-90 or set up for ranch and oilfield travel, we bring diagnostics-first repair (DVOM / thermal imaging where needed) to you. Billings sees diesel pushers and fifth-wheels staging for the Bozeman / Yellowstone push, plus truck campers and towables used for working travel.

Mountain-grade driving west of here means cooling stress, chassis fault codes, and brake wear show up more than on flat interstate. Generators that sat through a Montana winter often need surge or no-start diagnosis before the next long leg.

Common Billings-area calls

Diagnostics & chassis

Chassis, cooling, and brake diagnostics for Ford, GM, Sprinter, and Freightliner platforms. Pre-trip checks before the mountain grades toward Yellowstone.

Power & house systems

Generator service (Onan, Generac, Kohler, NPS), electrical / Victron battery systems, plumbing and holding tanks, roof seals with thermal imaging, LP gas safety checks.
