Pricing

Price guide

Transparent field pricing for mobile RV repair — same figures as our main pricing page, written as a quick reference before you book.

United Mobile RV LLC quotes trip fees upfront. You are not calling a handyman — you get diagnostic-first mobile service with clear labor increments and flat seasonal packages.

Core rates

  • Labor — ~$150/hr, 1-hour minimum, then billed in 30-minute increments. Parts and materials separate.
  • Trip / service call — $75 within 30 miles; beyond that $75 + $1.50/mi each way from the on-route base.
  • Diagnostic — $150 full-system scan (DVOM / thermal where needed), applied toward repair if you proceed.

Flat-rate seasonal

  • Winterization — $175 fixed (blow-out, antifreeze, water heater bypass, system check). Labor included; materials extra if needed.
  • Trip prep & safety check — $225 fixed (brakes, lights, tires, hitch, chains, breakaway, LP check). Labor included; materials extra if needed.

These are separate line items — not coach-class splits. Electrical, appliance, plumbing, roof/seal, and Victron/solar builds are quoted from the work — see full pricing and price list.
