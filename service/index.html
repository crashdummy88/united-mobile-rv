How we work

Not sure what you need? Start with the symptom — a draining battery, a roof stain, a generator that won't take load — and pick the matching service. Every visit starts with a proper operational test and root-cause diagnosis before parts get swapped.

One tech shows up with professional tooling: thermal imaging, DVOM, combustible gas leak detection, and manufacturer-aligned commissioning software for Victron and Peplink systems.

Labor ~$150/hr. Diagnostic $150 applied toward repair. Trip $75 / 30 mi then $1.50/mi — quoted upfront before we roll.

Shop-lift rebuilds, paint booths, and major frame work stay shop territory. On everything else we can do correctly in the field, we stay until the system holds.
