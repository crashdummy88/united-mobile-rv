Platforms

Built-in coach units and portables. Onan QG/QD/Marquis (gas and LP) dominate the fleet; Generac, Kohler, NPS (Yamaha-based inverter gens), and portables (Honda, Champion, Westinghouse, and similar) are in scope for diagnosis and field repair.

Service coverage

What we diagnose and fix

No-start diagnosis

Fuel delivery, ignition, choke, starter, and control-board signals — carb fouling and fuel degradation after storage are common.

Surging & unstable output

Carburetor / governor issues that hunt RPM or produce unstable voltage under load.

Overload & shutdown

Low output, failing AVR, brush wear, or circuit overload when A/C comes online.

Oil & fuel service

Oil, air filter, plugs, fuel filter — ethanol-blended fuel left sitting is a primary failure driver.

Brushes, AVR, fault codes

Wear items in the generator head plus Onan fault-code interpretation in context.

Exhaust & mounting

Exhaust leaks are a CO hazard; connections, muffler, and compartment sealing checked on service visits.

Schedules

Keep it reliable

Annual service

Oil, filter, plug, fuel filter, and operational test under load — the baseline between seasons.

Pre-storage

Fuel treatment or carb drain before long sits.

Post-storage start-up

Inspect and service before you need it on a 95°F campsite afternoon.
