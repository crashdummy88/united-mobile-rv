Pre-purchase inspection (PPI)

Systematic, documented assessment before you sign — thermal imaging, electrical, LP, plumbing, appliances, and more. Sellers rarely volunteer deferred maintenance.

Active corridor: Montana · Wyoming · Idaho · Washington — case-by-case beyond.

Why it matters

Used coaches can look sharp and still hide seasons of roof leaks, a failing converter, or a water heater that hasn't run on electric in a year. Inspection cost is a fraction of one major undisclosed repair.

Coverage

What the inspection includes

Roof & exterior

Penetrations, seams, membrane, sidewalls, windows, doors, slide seals — close-up.

Thermal imaging

Hidden moisture in walls, ceilings, and floors without destructive teardown.

Electrical

Shore inlet, transfer switch, converter stages, battery load test, 12V panel, GFCI.

LP gas

Regulator pressure, supply lines, leak detection at every connection, appliance feeds.

Plumbing

Pressurized leak hold, pump operation, valves, visible drains and traps.

Appliances & generator

Fridge gas/electric, furnace, water heater, A/C; generator under load with codes when equipped.

Slides, awnings, interior

Full cycle, seal compression, soft spots, intrusion evidence, vents/fans.

Chassis or trailer systems

Motorhome fluids/belts/codes/brakes; towables get 7-pin, magnets, breakaway, coupler.

Deliverable

Written report of systems checked and findings, with photos/thermal images of significant issues. Plain-language walkthrough of what it means and rough cost to address — so you negotiate or walk with eyes open.

Schedule before an offer, after acceptance before closing, or post-purchase to baseline a unit you already bought.

Trip fee $75 within 30 miles, then $1.50/mi. Labor ~$150/hr. Diagnostic $150 applied toward repair if you proceed. Parts billed separately.
