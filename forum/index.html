📌 Community Rules

🤝 Be useful, be kind

Answer like you'd want answered. No putting down someone for not knowing something — everyone started somewhere.

🚫 No spam or self-promo

Selling something or dropping links unrelated to the question gets your post held for review automatically, and removed by a moderator.

⚙️ Keep it on-topic

Repair, off-grid/power, connectivity, route & service areas, or general RV/vanlife talk. Political and unrelated content isn't for this forum.

🤖 Posts are AI-screened

New threads and replies are auto-checked for spam, scams, and abuse before going live. Most posts clear instantly — flagged ones wait for a human moderator.

⚠️ Not a substitute for hands-on diagnosis

Advice here (including from us) is offered in good faith, but isn't a replacement for an in-person look at your rig before you act on anything electrical, gas, or structural.

Start a thread
