How to ask

Write the thread the way you would want to answer it.

This board is owner-to-owner and technician-to-owner Q&A. It is not a checkout and it is not an appointment desk. Sign in with Google to post. New threads and replies are screened for spam and scams before they appear.

Title the symptom

“2019 Allegro — converter fan never stops” gets a better answer than “electrical help??”. Year, brand, and the thing that failed belong in the title.

Say what you already tried

Swaps, resets, and settings changes. Duplicate advice wastes everyone’s time. If you have not tried anything yet, say that too.

Stay in the right topic

Repair & Diagnostics for a failed system. Off-Grid & Power for bank / solar / inverter builds. Connectivity for Starlink, weBoost, Peplink. Route for corridor talk. Updates for shop notes.

One problem per thread

A dead house bank and a roof leak are two threads. Mixing them buries the answer and the next owner cannot find it.

Evidence in the post

Give the thread something to work from.

Guessing in the replies usually costs someone a good converter, pump, or antenna. Put the evidence in the first post so answers can be specific. Photos belong on the thread — up to four per post.

Repair threads

When it started, what changed, any fault code, and a photo of the panel or the component label. “It just died” is a start; a code and a model stamp is an answerable question.

Power threads

Bank chemistry, inverter or converter model, solar controller, and roughly what you run overnight. A GX screenshot or a label photo beats “I have lithium.”

Connectivity threads

Dish vs booster vs router, sky view, and whether the failure is no-lock, slow, or failover flapping. App screenshots help more than a brand name.

Route threads

Where you are headed and when. This topic is corridor talk among owners — it is not how a visit is scheduled.

Advice here is offered in good faith by other members and by the technician when he replies. It is not a substitute for standing in front of the rig. Smell of LP, live 120V you cannot isolate, or a soft roof deck: stop the thread and text (616) 606-5277.

Community rules

How this forum is run

Be useful

Answer the way you would want an answer. Do not put someone down for not knowing a system — everyone started somewhere.

No spam or unrelated promotion

Selling something or dropping links unrelated to the question holds the post for review and can get it removed.

Stay on topic

Repair, off-grid power, connectivity, route and service areas, or general RV and van talk. Political and unrelated content is not for this forum.

Posts are screened

New threads and replies are checked for spam, scams, and abuse before they go live. Most posts clear quickly. Flagged ones wait for a human moderator.

Not an appointment desk

Do not treat a thread as a work order. If you need someone on site, use Book or text. If you need a parts stack specified, use Shop. This page is for questions and answers.
