Troubleshoot

weBoost: no boost / keeps cutting out

Outdoor RF and antenna isolation first — boosters don’t invent signal from zero.

United Mobile RV is a weBoost Authorized Installer. If indoor bars never improve, either the donor hears nothing usable or oscillation is forcing AGC to slam gain down.

Quick path

  1. Measure usable outdoor signal at the donor antenna location (no outdoor RF = no honest boost).
  2. Increase indoor/outdoor antenna separation; remove line-of-sight between them.
  3. Check coax damage, loose connectors, and power/fuse/polarity at the amp.
  4. Watch for oscillation symptoms: amp throttling, worse performance than no booster.
  5. Retest after travel — vibration moves antennas and connectors.
