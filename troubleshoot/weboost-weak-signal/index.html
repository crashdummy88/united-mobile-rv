Troubleshoot · Pack 3

weBoost weak signal

A cellular booster amplifies usable outdoor signal into the coach. It will not invent RF from zero, and “more gain” is not a knob we turn — weBoost uses AGC. Prefer Text for Authorized Installer diagnostics.

United Mobile RV is a weBoost Authorized Installer.

Symptoms

  • Interior bars barely move after install; calls still drop
  • Booster LED / app shows oscillation, overload, or error states
  • Works near the window, fails deeper in the coach
  • Worked parked, fails after travel (antenna shift, coax strain)
  • Hot booster or power supply; Peplink cellular WAN still flaps with booster “on”

Quick checks (owner-safe)

  1. Outside first: booster off — any usable outdoor signal at the exterior antenna? No outdoor signal → cannot invent coverage.
  2. LED / app status: photo exact lights or messages (oscillation / overload).
  3. Separation: exterior/interior isolation per OEM — oscillation is often geometry.
  4. Coax & connectors: kinks, water, loose fittings after travel.
  5. Power: fused DC; no shared circuit that sags under inverter load.
  6. Carrier reality: bars ≠ throughput; outdoor vs indoor tests on/off.

When to stop DIY

  • Seal failure / spinning roof mounts; water in coax; hot/melting supplies
  • Cutting proprietary antenna cables without OEM guidance
  • Plans to “crank gain” or flash non-OEM firmware

Outdoor signal required

Nulls (canyon, dense timber, metal carport) give the booster nothing useful to amplify. Moving the coach or accepting Starlink for that stretch often beats a second kit.

AGC — we don’t “tune gain”

weBoost uses automatic gain control. We place antennas and verify LEDs/app health — no promised bar counts.

Oscillation vs weak signal

  • Oscillation — antennas hearing each other; fix geometry / isolation.
  • Overload — too much outdoor signal near a tower; OEM attenuation / placement.
  • True weak signal — outdoor marginal; physics still wins in the hole.

Power and travel damage

Travel loosens mounts and chews coax. Flaky 12V looks like a dead booster — see 12V battery no charge.

Starlink + Peplink

Diagnose weBoost → Peplink cellular separately from Starlink, then WAN priority. Starlink / Peplink dropouts.

Honesty box

  • Needs usable outdoor signal — will not invent RF from zero
  • AGC — we don’t tune gain or promise bar counts
  • Official support only: weboost.com/support
