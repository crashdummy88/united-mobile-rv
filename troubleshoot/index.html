How to use

Symptom first, parts last

Pick the symptom card that matches what you see — dead house power, no water, a soft roof deck, a generator that won’t start, Starlink offline, weBoost with no boost, a flapping Peplink WAN, freeze damage, or a fridge that drops propane. Open that card, work the quick path, and capture photos, fault codes, app screenshots, and voltages before you book. These pages frame the diagnosis so fewer miles and parts get wasted; they are not a substitute for DVOM / thermal metering on a live coach.

Stay diagnostic-first. Forum guesses often replace a healthy converter, pump, regulator, or antenna and leave the real fault untouched. Use the symptom forks to clear power, fuel, RF, freeze, and safety gates first. When the quick path ends — or you hit a stop-DIY line (LP smell, live 120V you cannot isolate, structural soft deck, oscillating booster) — Prefer Text instead of forcing the next DIY step.

How the hub works

Diagnostic-first index, not a parts catalog

This hub is the mothership for symptom starters. Each card is a field path: what you see → quick checks → forks → when to stop DIY → what United Mobile RV meters on site. Below the cards, the system library deep-links longer guides (electrical, solar/battery, Victron codes, generator, LP, plumbing, roof, slides, connectivity). Use a card for a clear symptom; use a guide for system depth or OEM code context.

No competitor bake-in. This mothership is not live WordPress — Pages SoT for Prefer Text booking. Trip fee quoted before we roll; diagnostic applies to the repair when you authorize work. If DIY ends mid-path, text the card you were on plus photos — usually enough to triage mobile service in the active corridor.
