Troubleshoot

Starlink offline / crawling speeds

Check sky, power, and cable path before you assume the dish is dead.

Most “Starlink is broken” calls are obstruction, power brownout, or a cable/connector issue — not a failed dish. United Mobile RV installs Starlink hardware; we do not claim a Starlink certified dealer title.

Quick path

  1. Confirm clear sky — trees and eaves matter more than firmware myths.
  2. Verify dish + router are powered from a clean fused source (not a weak inverter brownout).
  3. Inspect cable ends and entry seals for water, crush, or intermittent continuity.
  4. Check the Starlink app obstruction / outage status before swapping hardware.
  5. If you dual-WAN with Peplink, confirm which path actually dropped (Starlink vs cellular).
