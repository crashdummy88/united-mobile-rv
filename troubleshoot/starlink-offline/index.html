Troubleshoot

Starlink offline / crawling speeds

Sky view, power, cable, and dual-WAN checks before you blame the dish. Installs-only honesty.

Most “Starlink is broken” calls are obstruction, power brownout, or a cable/connector issue — not a failed dish. United Mobile RV installs Starlink hardware; we do not claim a Starlink Certified dealer title — install capability only.

Symptoms

  • Starlink app: obstruction, offline, searching, or reboot loops
  • Works parked clear-sky; fails under trees / next to buildings / at highway speed
  • Wi-Fi OK locally but no internet
  • Boot loops after inverter surge, microwave, or AC kick-on (looks like ISP failure)
  • Cable / connector strain at roof boot after travel days
  • Dropouts that line up with low house-bank voltage
  • Dual-WAN with Peplink — unclear which path actually dropped

Quick path (owner-safe)

  1. Check the Starlink app obstruction map, outage status, and exact offline reason. Screenshot it before resetting anything twice.
  2. Confirm clear-sky: awning rails, AC shrouds, and trees will keep the dish searching.
  3. Power: dedicated fused DC / PoE path — not a shared circuit that sags when the microwave or AC kicks on.
  4. Cable/boot: gentle wiggle-test after travel days. Do not cut, splice, or lengthen proprietary Starlink cable without OEM guidance.
  5. If you dual-WAN with Peplink, confirm which WAN actually dropped (Starlink vs cellular) and Health Check status before the double-reset ritual.
  6. Reboot dish/router once; note firmware. Official app/support only — no sideload “fixes” or community flashes.
  7. If dropouts line up with heavy 12V loads, meter house voltage at the bank under that load — brownout often masquerades as ISP failure.

Symptom forks

  • Obstruction / searching — mount placement and clear-sky first. Obstruction maps do not lie.
  • Offline after heavy 12V load — supply sag looking like an ISP failure; fix power before chasing RF.
  • Works parked, fails rolling — vibration, cable strain, or dual-WAN priority flap.
  • Wi-Fi OK, no internet — WAN/path down; check app offline reason and Peplink WAN status.
  • Reboot loops — PoE injector heat, weak DC feed, or thermal shutdown — stop-DIY if connectors are melted.

Starlink path — obstruction and mount

A dish tucked under an awning rail or beside an AC shroud will search forever in tree cover. Weatherproof exterior mount, sealed penetrations, and clean cable routing are the install standard — clear-sky assessed first.

Power and boot loops

PoE injectors and dish supplies that share a weak circuit will brown out when the microwave or AC kicks on. Confirm fused DC, proper gauge, and that the injector is not heat-damaged. Melted connectors are a stop-DIY moment — Prefer Text.

When to stop DIY

  • Loose / spinning roof mount or seal failure risk
  • PoE injector heat damage or melted DC connectors
  • Water at the cable boot; any penetration you cannot reseal
  • Community firmware flashes or cable lengthening without OEM guidance

What UMRT diagnoses

Clear-sky mount and cable routing (Starlink install capability only). Power stability under load (DVOM where needed). Dual-WAN interaction with Peplink Health Check / failover as configured. Honest expectation-setting: trees, canyons, and weather outages are physics — not always a remount.
