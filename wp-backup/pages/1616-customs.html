Power Systems for Custom Built Vans & Skoolies

The power system is the most important and most complex part of any van or skoolie build. Get it right and you have a reliable, capable off-grid setup. Get it wrong and you have a system that underperforms, fails prematurely, or creates a fire hazard.

System Design & Load Analysis

Every build starts with a power audit — what you're actually running, how long you run it, and what your charging sources are. From that we size the battery bank, solar array, and inverter/charger correctly for your real usage rather than a generic estimate.

LiFePO4 Battery Banks

Higher usable capacity, faster charge acceptance, longer cycle life, and significant weight savings over AGM. We size the bank to your needs, handle BMS integration, and verify charge parameter compatibility across every charging source in the system.

Victron Inverter/Charger Installation

The Victron MultiPlus and Quattro series are the gold standard for mobile off-grid power. We handle the full installation — AC input and output wiring, DC connections, transfer switching, and complete programming through VEConfigure. Every parameter set correctly for your battery chemistry and usage profile.

MPPT Solar Controllers

Solar panel array wiring, combiner boxes, appropriate fusing, and Victron MPPT controller installation and programming. Charge profile matched to your battery bank. String configuration optimized for your roof layout and shading conditions.

DC-DC Chargers

Charging your house battery bank from your alternator while driving is an important part of a complete charging strategy — especially in low-sun conditions. We install Victron Orion DC-DC chargers sized correctly for your alternator capacity and integrated properly with your battery management system.

Shore Power Integration

A proper shore power inlet, appropriate amperage wiring, and inverter/charger configuration that handles the transition between shore power and battery/inverter operation seamlessly. Input current limiting set correctly so you don't trip breakers at campgrounds with limited service.

Victron VRM Remote Monitoring

Every Victron system we install gets configured for VRM portal access — real-time visibility into your battery state of charge, solar production, power consumption, and system status from your phone. You always know what your system is doing.

Additional Systems

12V DC Wiring & Distribution

Properly sized wire runs, appropriate fusing at every circuit, a clean distribution panel, and labeled circuits throughout. We don't run undersized wire, we don't skip fuses, and we don't leave wire runs unsecured.

Fresh Water Systems

12V demand pump installation, fresh water tank integration, PEX supply line runs to sinks and showers, pressure regulator for city water connection, and inline filtration. Built to work reliably and winterize when needed.

LP Gas Systems

Propane tank mounting, regulator installation, copper supply line runs, appliance connections, and full leak detection and pressure testing before sign-off. Every LP installation is tested with a manometer and combustible gas detector.

Diesel & Propane Heating

Webasto, Espar, and diesel air heater installation and wiring. Propane heating system installation. Proper exhaust routing, combustion air intake placement, and control wiring. Heat that works reliably in cold weather is not optional for full-time living.

How We Work on Build Projects

Build projects are quoted per project after a consultation to understand the scope, the vehicle, and what systems are being installed. We work in stages if needed — power system first, then water, then gas — or tackle everything at once depending on the build timeline.

We document every system we install. Wire runs labeled and mapped, component settings recorded, and a system overview provided at project completion. Every installation is tested fully before we sign off — power system under load, water system pressurized and leak-checked, LP system pressure tested, heating system cycled through full operation.
