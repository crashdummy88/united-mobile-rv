Where We Travel

United Mobile RV LLC operates a scheduled, multi-state service route rather than a fixed shop location. Our currently active service corridor runs through Montana, Wyoming, Idaho, and Washington — with certified diagnostic and repair service available at each hub along the way. Michigan, Wisconsin, and South Dakota remain on our route and are served case by case as we cycle back through.
