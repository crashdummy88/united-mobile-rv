We don't turn down work because it's complicated, because it requires diagnostic equipment, or because it takes longer than expected. We show up prepared, diagnose it properly, and fix it correctly.

Common Repairs We Handle

Interior & Living Systems

Cabinet hardware, drawer slides, door latches, and hinges. Flooring repairs and subfloor issues. Window and vent repairs. Ceiling fan and overhead light replacements. Blinds, shades, and interior trim. The small things that add up and make daily life in an RV frustrating — all worth fixing.

Electrical

Blown fuses, tripped breakers, failed outlets, flickering lights, dead switches, and wiring issues of all kinds. Shore power problems, battery issues, converter faults, and 12V system failures. If it runs on electricity inside your RV, we can diagnose and repair it.

Plumbing

Leaking faucets, failed water pumps, broken fresh water lines, holding tank valve issues, and slow drains. Winterization and de-winterization. Any water system problem from the tank to the tap.

Appliances

Refrigerators, furnaces, air conditioners, and water heaters that aren't working properly. We diagnose and repair all major RV appliance brands on-site with proper electrical and gas testing.

Roof & Exterior

Sealant failures, roof membrane issues, water intrusion, and exterior seal inspection. Catching these early is the difference between a quick repair and an expensive structural problem.

LP Gas

Pressure testing, leak detection, regulator inspection, and appliance feed verification. Gas system integrity is a safety issue — it gets checked properly every time.

Mechanical & Chassis

Belts, hoses, cooling system issues, engine fault codes, and chassis-related problems on motorhome platforms. We bring automotive diagnostic capability to RV chassis work that most RV techs simply don't have.
