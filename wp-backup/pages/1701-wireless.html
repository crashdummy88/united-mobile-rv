5G / 4G Cellular

Cellular networks are fast, low-latency, and work well anywhere tower coverage is strong. For travelers who stay in or near urban and suburban areas, a quality cellular booster and antenna setup often delivers the best experience — no subscription hardware to manage, and performance improves as carriers expand their networks.

We install weBoost cellular boosters with proper antenna placement, clean coax routing, and DC power integration. Not zip-tied to a ladder rack — mounted, grounded, and done right.

Starlink

SpaceX's low-Earth orbit satellite network covers areas where no cell tower exists — making it the go-to solution for full-time travelers, boondockers, and anyone spending time in truly remote locations.

We handle full exterior Starlink dish mounting with weatherproof installation, interior cable routing, and power integration into your coach's DC or shore power system. Clean penetrations, proper sealing, no exposed runs.

Why Installation Quality Matters

A booster bolted to a ladder rack with exposed coax is a different product than one properly mounted, grounded, and integrated with your coach's DC power system. Antenna placement, cable routing, connector quality, and power delivery all affect real-world performance.
