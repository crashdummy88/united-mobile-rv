Service

General mobile RV repair

When the problem doesn't fit a neat category — or you just know something isn't right — diagnostic-first mobile repair at your campsite, driveway, or storage yard.

Active corridor: Montana · Wyoming · Idaho · Washington — case-by-case beyond.

What “general” means here

Not low-skill filler work. 11+ years across automotive, aerospace, and equipment maintenance means we don't bounce complicated jobs. We show up prepared, diagnose properly, and fix correctly — or tell you honestly when a shop lift is required.

Common calls

Across the coach

Interior & living

Hardware, latches, flooring/subfloor issues, windows, vents, fans, blinds.

Electrical

Fuses, breakers, outlets, lights, switches, shore power, batteries, converters, 12V failures.

Plumbing

Faucets, pumps, lines, valves, drains — plus winterization / de-winterization.

Appliances

Fridge, furnace, A/C, water heater — major brands, electrical and gas testing.

Roof & exterior

Sealant failures, membrane issues, intrusion — catch early.

LP, chassis, mechanical

Pressure/leak checks; belts, hoses, cooling, engine codes on motorhome platforms.

Who we work on

Class A/B/C motorhomes (gas and diesel), travel trailers and fifth wheels, Sprinter/Transit/ProMaster conversions, toy haulers, and skoolies — especially electrical and power systems.

Labor ~$150/hr (1-hour minimum). Parts separate. Cost framed before work starts. If it needs a shop, we say so upfront.

Pricing teaser

Trip fee $75 within 30 miles, then $1.50/mi. Labor ~$150/hr (1-hour minimum, billed in 30-minute increments). Diagnostic $150 applied toward repair if you proceed. Parts billed separately.

Credentials (text only): Victron Professional Certified Installer · weBoost Authorized Installer · Peplink Certified Associate · Dometic Professional Certified.
