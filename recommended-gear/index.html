Buyer's guide

What to look for, by category

Specific picks and links are coming as our affiliate accounts clear vendor review. Until then, here's what actually matters when you're shopping each category yourself.

Lithium (LiFePO4) batteries

Built-in BMS with low-temp charge cutoff, a real amp-hour rating (not a marketing number), and a form factor that fits your existing battery bay without custom fab.

Solar charge controllers

MPPT over PWM for anything beyond a trickle panel. Size it to your array's actual watts, not your battery bank — undersizing throttles harvest on short winter days.

Solar panels

Rigid panels outlast flexible ones on a roof that takes branches and hail; flexible only makes sense on curved or weight-sensitive mounts. Check the real STC wattage, not the "up to."

Inverters & inverter/chargers

Pure sine wave only if you're running anything with a motor or a sensitive charger. Combined inverter/chargers save wiring complexity over separate units.

Roof coatings

Match the coating chemistry to your existing roof membrane (EPDM, TPO, fiberglass) — the wrong pairing peels within a season. Clean and fully cure before a second coat.

LP & CO detectors

Replace on the manufacturer's stated service life, not when it starts chirping — sensors degrade silently before they fail loud. Combo LP/CO units simplify wiring on a retrofit.

Macerator pumps

Look at amp draw against your house battery budget and real gallons-per-minute under load, not the box number. Serviceable blade housings save a full replacement later.

Composting toilets

Bin capacity vs. how many people/days between empties is the real spec that matters — not the brand. Confirm your bathroom's ventilation can support one before buying.

12V fridges

Compressor-based (not absorption) for reliable off-grid cooling and functioning at an angle. Check actual continuous amp draw against your battery bank, not peak startup draw.
