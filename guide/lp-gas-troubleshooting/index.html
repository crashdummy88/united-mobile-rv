Field notes

LP gas problems are safety-sensitive by nature. Here’s how to tell a routine fix from something that needs a professional immediately — plus the regulator, tank, and detector reference every owner should know. If you smell gas, hear hissing, or your LP or CO detector alarms, shut off the supply valve at the tank, evacuate, and do not operate any switches, lighters, or appliances until the system has been checked by a professional. Low Flame, Yellow Flame, or Soot

A healthy propane flame burns blue. Yellow or orange flame, visible soot, or a weak flame all point to a combustion or pressure problem — not something to ignore.
