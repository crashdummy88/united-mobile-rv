50-amp service isn’t “more current on one wire” — it’s a fundamentally different hookup. Here’s what that means for what you can actually run. A 30-amp shore connection is a single 120V leg — roughly 3,600 watts total available. A 50-amp connection is two separate 120V legs (100 amps total across both) — roughly 12,000 watts available, more than triple.
