Field notes

An undersized inverter trips constantly. An oversized one wastes standby power every hour of every day. Size it to what you actually run. Every AC device draws a running wattage, but motor-driven devices (compressor fridges, microwaves, power tools, some water pumps) draw a much higher surge for the first fraction of a second on startup — often 2–3x the running wattage.
