Coleman Mach AC Troubleshooting Guide

The other major rooftop AC brand alongside Dometic — different control board, different common faults.

Active corridor: Montana · Wyoming · Idaho · Washington — case-by-case beyond.

Field notes

The other major rooftop AC brand alongside Dometic — different control board, different common faults. Check pedestal/generator voltage first — Coleman Mach compressors have a start-capacitor circuit sensitive to low voltage, and many “dead compressor” calls turn out to be a sagging 30-amp connection rather than the AC unit itself. If voltage is solid, a failed start capacitor or run capacitor is the next most common cause.

Fan Runs, Compressor Won’t Kick On

Check pedestal/generator voltage first — Coleman Mach compressors have a start-capacitor circuit sensitive to low voltage, and many “dead compressor” calls turn out to be a sagging 30-amp connection rather than the AC.

Unit Cycles On and Off Rapidly

Usually a dirty condenser coil or a frozen evaporator coil restricting airflow — both trip the thermal overload protection built into the compressor.

Thermostat Not Responding
