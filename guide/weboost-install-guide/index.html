Field notes

United Mobile RV is a weBoost Authorized Installer. A booster amplifies usable outdoor signal — it does not invent coverage from zero RF. Modern systems use automatic gain control (AGC); we place antennas and cable so AGC works, we do not “tune gain” like a volume knob.

Three parts, one system

Outdoor (donor) antenna, amplifier, and indoor antenna must be spaced and aimed as a system. Cheap cable, crushed runs, and random ground-plane choices waste as much performance as a wrong amp model.

Outdoor RF required

If the donor antenna hears nothing usable, the amp has nothing honest to boost. Dead canyons and metal sheds are physics problems, not “need a bigger booster” problems. We check outdoor signal before promising indoor bars.

AGC & oscillation

If indoor and outdoor antennas hear each other, the amp re-amplifies its own output. AGC then slams gain down — performance can be worse than no booster. Separation, isolation, and correct interior panel placement keep AGC from fighting you.

Power & fuse

Fused DC power, clean grounds, and travel-safe mounting beat a booster zip-tied to a ladder with exposed coax. Vibration and UV destroy sloppy installs on the first long corridor day.

With Starlink / Peplink

weBoost helps weak-but-present cell. Starlink covers no-tower country. Peplink bonding and/or failover ties the WANs together. Antenna clusters need physical isolation so systems don’t interfere.

When DIY ends

Oscillation you can’t clear, roof penetrations, or “works in the driveway / dies at boondock” after a self-install usually means placement and path — not a random Amazon antenna swap. Prefer Text with photos of outdoor/indoor antenna locations.

Owner checklist
  • Measure outdoor signal at candidate donor locations before drilling
  • Maximize indoor/outdoor antenna separation and avoid line-of-sight between them
  • Use specified coax; don’t coil spare cable next to the amp
  • Fuse the DC feed at the source; verify polarity
  • Retest after travel — vibration moves antennas and loosens connectors
