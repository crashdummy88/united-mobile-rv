weBoost install — Authorized Installer, AGC honesty

United Mobile RV is a weBoost Authorized Installer. We place donor and indoor antennas to reduce oscillation and let automatic gain control (AGC) do its job — we don’t promise magic bars where outdoor RF isn’t there.

Active corridor: Montana · Wyoming · Idaho · Washington — case-by-case beyond.

Field notes

A cell booster amplifies whatever weak signal already reaches the donor antenna. Install it wrong and AGC will throttle gain — or you’ll get oscillation feedback that makes performance worse than no booster.

Three parts, one system

Outdoor (donor) antenna, amplifier, and indoor antenna must be spaced and aimed as a system. Cable quality and ground-plane choices matter as much as the amp model.

AGC & oscillation

Automatic gain control (AGC) reduces boost when the amp hears its own output or when outdoor signal is already strong. Separation and isolation keep AGC from slamming gain to the floor.

Authorized Installer

weBoost Authorized Installer work means placement and signal optimization — not “guaranteed bars” in a dead canyon. Outdoor RF and clear sightlines still win.
