Field notes

United Mobile RV is a Peplink Certified Associate. Multi-WAN done right means honest design: bonding and/or failover with SpeedFusion where the hardware and plan support it — not “bonding-only” marketing when the site only needs clean failover.

Bonding vs failover

Failover switches to a backup WAN when the primary dies. Bonding aggregates multiple WANs for throughput and resilience when radios, SIMs, and the subscription allow it. We size to how you travel — not a generic max-speed pitch.

Starlink + cellular

Common pair: Starlink for bulk bandwidth, cellular for low latency or tree cover. Peplink sits in the middle with clean WAN wiring, SIM/modem paths, and LAN/Wi‑Fi that survives travel.

SpeedFusion expectations

SpeedFusion is Peplink’s bonding/failover technology family. What you get depends on model, firmware, and licenses — we commission what’s actually on the invoice, not a brochure feature list.

Antennas & isolation

Cellular antennas, Starlink dish, and weBoost outdoor antennas need separation. Crowding everything on one corner of the roof creates interference and service calls that look like “bad firmware.”

Power & cabinet

Routers want clean DC, airflow, and strain-relieved cabling. A Peplink bouncing in a plastic bin behind the sofa is not a mobile install.

When DIY ends

Intermittent WAN flaps, SIM auth loops, or SpeedFusion tunnels that never stabilize after a self-wire usually need metered WAN tests and antenna path checks on site. Prefer Text with model + photo of the WAN wiring.

Owner checklist
  • Know whether you need failover only or true bonding before you buy radios/SIMs
  • Label WAN ports and SIM slots — future you will thank present you
  • Update firmware from official Peplink downloads only (see umrt-software /peplink/)
  • Test failover by intentionally dropping each WAN before you leave the driveway
  • Keep antenna coax out of AC power bundles and sharp chassis edges
