Solar guide

Solar & battery troubleshooting

When harvest is low or the bank won't stay charged, separate array, controller, wiring, and battery chemistry before you buy more panels.

Field notes

Check array shading and dirty glass, string voltage into the controller, MPPT vs PWM behavior in variable light, wire gauge/voltage drop, fuse/breaker health, and BMS/charge parameters matched to LiFePO4 vs AGM. VRM or controller logs often show the fault faster than guesswork.

Array & controller

Input voltage/current vs expected; MPPT tracking; temperature derate.

Battery & BMS

Load test capacity; confirm charge voltages match chemistry; watch for BMS disconnects.

Wiring & fusing

Undersized runs and loose lugs look like "bad solar."

Commissioned fixes

Victron Professional installs — sizing, programming, and VRM when you want it done once.
