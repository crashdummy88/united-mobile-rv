Owner checklist
  • Check the campground pedestal breaker first — it may have tripped
  • Inspect your shore power cord for burnt or melted pins at both ends
  • Check your coach breaker panel — reset tripped breakers, but if one trips again there’s an underlying cause
  • Test your 30/50 amp adapter — adapters corrode and fail more often than people expect
  • Verify pedestal voltage (120V AC) — anything under 108V AC can damage appliances silently over time
