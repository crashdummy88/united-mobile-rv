Connectivity guide

Starlink for RVs — mount, power, and routing

Installs-only: roof or portable mount, cable routing, clean power, and seal integrity. Clear sky required. Not a Starlink “certified” dealer title.

Active corridor: Montana · Wyoming · Idaho · Washington — case-by-case beyond.

Field notes

Starlink changed remote work on the road — but an RV install is electrical and roof work, not “plug the dish in and hope.” United Mobile RV installs and integrates Starlink hardware (mount, seals, cable, power). We do not claim a Starlink “certified” dealer title.

Clear sky first

Starlink needs a clear view of the sky. Trees, building eaves, and tall roof clutter kill performance. Assess the park spot and mount location before you cut a hole. Portable kits trade convenience for setup time every stop; roof mounts win for full-timers who park with sky.

Roof mount & seals

Weatherproof exterior mount, sealed penetrations, and cable entry that survive UV and travel flex. Wrong sealant or an unsealed chase becomes a water intrusion call six months later. We treat penetrations like any other roof job — sealed, documented, travel-safe.

Cable routing

Route cable so it doesn’t chafe on sharp metal, trap water, or get crushed by slides. Interior runs should look intentional — no ladder-rack disasters, no unprotected coax across the roof membrane.

Clean power

Dish + router draw continuous power that belongs in your off-grid budget. Feed from a clean fused DC source (or proper inverter path) — not a daisy-chained cigarette socket. Messy house DC / fuse-bus cleanup is power work and should be scoped separately from the Starlink hours.

Pair with cellular

Starlink is bulk bandwidth when towers disappear. Cellular still wins for low latency near coverage. Many travelers run both — Peplink multi-WAN bonding and/or failover (SpeedFusion) is the clean way to combine them. See the Peplink guide.

When DIY ends

Stop at sealed roof penetrations you aren’t equipped to warranty, unknown DC fuse hierarchy, or intermittent boot loops after a DIY power tap. Prefer Text with roof photos + coach year/make — we quote trip fee before we roll.

Owner checklist
  • Check obstruction map / sky view before committing to a roof location
  • Confirm plan type (roam vs residential) matches how you travel
  • Budget continuous watts for dish + router in your lithium/inverter math
  • Never leave an unsealed roof penetration “for later”
  • If pairing with cell, plan antenna isolation before you mount everything in one cluster
