Starlink for RVs — mount, power, and routing

Satellite internet changed remote work on the road — but the RV-specific plan, mounting choice, and power draw have to match how you actually travel. United Mobile RV installs and integrates Starlink hardware the same way we do electrical work. We are not a Starlink “certified” dealer.

Active corridor: Montana · Wyoming · Idaho · Washington — case-by-case beyond.

Field notes

Clear sky view still wins outdoors — trees and canyon walls beat any mount choice. A poorly sealed roof penetration is one of the most common sources of interior water damage, so this install is worth doing carefully. Dish + router draw meaningful continuous power; budget them with fridge and other always-on loads, not as negligible.

Roof-mount vs portable

Roof mount

Hands-off every time you park. Needs clear sky overhead and a sealed penetration done right — solid, level base and cable routing that does not create a water intrusion point. Not a job to rush.

Portable dish

Flexible under trees; takes setup time and a secure cable run back to the coach. Useful when the roof position is shaded or you change sites often.

Plan reality

RV / roaming plans matter when you change locations frequently rather than parking long-term at one address. Match the plan to how you travel — we install hardware; account/plan details stay with the customer and Starlink.

Optional Peplink

Want cellular failover or multi-WAN bonding alongside Starlink? Peplink Certified Associate installs for bonding and/or failover — see the Peplink multi-WAN guide and /wireless/.

Power & cable

Off-grid budget

Include Starlink continuous draw in the daily watt-hour math with fridge, lights, and other always-on loads. See the off-grid power budget guide before locking battery bank size.

Fused integration

We route and fuse the feed so the install is part of the electrical system, not a dangling brick. Strain-relieve connectors; vibration opens mystery outages.

Seals & penetrations

Drip loops, chafe protection, and proper sealant at every roof penetration. A fast “temporary” hole becomes a soft floor two seasons later.

When DIY ends

New roof penetrations, sealed mounts, and coach DC integration. If the dish is offline after a move, start at Starlink offline troubleshoot.

Owner checklist
  • Assess clear-sky at typical parks before committing to a permanent roof mount
  • Budget continuous dish + router draw in your off-grid plan
  • Inspect roof seals at the mount seasonally
  • Keep firmware/app updates from official Starlink sources (software twin indexes links)
  • If you also run cellular, plan antenna separation and consider Peplink for one coach network

What good looks like on the road

After a stop

Roof mount: park, wait for acquire, confirm apps work before you unload the day. Portable: known cable path, secure footing, no trip hazard across the door.

Obstruction honesty

Partial tree cover can look “fine” on a map and still kill uplink. If the app shows heavy obstruction at your usual parks, fix placement before blaming the router or Peplink.

Heat and weather

Roof mounts see sun and freeze-thaw. Re-check fasteners and sealant after the first hard season. Cable jackets crack at chafe points first.

One coach network

Phones hopping between Starlink Wi‑Fi and a separate hotspot is fragile. Peplink (or a deliberate single SSID design) keeps devices on one LAN while WANs change underneath.

FAQ

Standard vs RV plan?

Roaming / RV-oriented plans matter when you change locations frequently. We install hardware; plan choice stays with you and Starlink.

How much power off-grid?

Meaningful continuous draw — include it in the budget with other always-on loads. Use the off-grid power budget guide for daily watt-hour math.

Is roof-mount safe from leaks?

Only with a properly sealed penetration and cable routing. A rushed mount is a common water-intrusion source.

Are you Starlink certified?

No. We are installs-only for Starlink. Certified partner titles we do claim: Victron, weBoost Authorized Installer, Peplink Certified Associate, Dometic.
