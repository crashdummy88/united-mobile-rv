How water gets in

Failed sealants at penetrations, membrane punctures/delamination, and slide-out seals are the usual paths. By the time you see a ceiling stain or soft floor, the intrusion has often been active for seasons.

Inspection

What a proper roof visit covers

Roof surface & membrane

Walk-on inspection for punctures, tears, soft spots, lift, and oxidation across EPDM, TPO, and fiberglass.

Penetrations & seams

Vents, antennas, A/C, fridge vents, skylights, cap seams — sealant cracking, shrinkage, and edge lift checked close-up.

Sidewalls, windows, doors

Exterior caulk joints where water sits and works into frames and compartments.

Slide-out seals

Roof wipers, side seals, floor seals — checked extended and retracted; intact-looking seals can still be torn when compressed.

Thermal imaging

FLIR differentials that flag moisture-saturated insulation and substrate without tearing walls apart.

Moisture meter confirmation

Thermal shows where to look; calibrated meters confirm what's actually wet.

Repairs

What we fix

Seal & lap sealant

Failed sealant removed completely before new material — never over cracked junk that traps moisture.

EPDM repairs

Punctures and tears patched correctly; larger delamination scoped honestly.

Vent & accessory reseals

Flanges verified sound before resealing fans, vents, and roof accessories.

Slide seal replacement

Correct profile, verified compression after install.

Most manufacturers want roof penetrations inspected at least yearly. Standalone inspections available when you are not ready for a full repair day.
