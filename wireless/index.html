Connectivity

Most serious travelers end up running both cellular and Starlink. Cell for low-latency coverage near towers; Starlink where towers disappear. We install both — and we talk through travel patterns so the setup matches how you actually roam.

What we install

Done right, not zip-tied

Starlink dish mounting

Weatherproof exterior mount, sealed penetrations, cable routing, and DC/shore power integration. Clear-sky position assessed first.

weBoost cellular boosters

weBoost Authorized Installer — exterior/interior antenna placement, proper coax, fused DC power. Needs usable outdoor signal; won't invent RF from zero. weBoost uses AGC (we don't "tune gain").

Peplink mobile routers

Peplink Certified Associate — multi-WAN bonding and/or failover (SpeedFusion) with Starlink + cellular for travelers who need resilient connectivity. Bonding ≠ simple failover — we design for both when the site needs it.

Roof antenna mounts

Low-profile, sealed into existing roof structure; positioned to limit drag and obstruction.

Interior wiring & power

Clean runs, proper fusing, coach DC integration. No exposed wire, no ladder-rack disasters.

Why quality matters

A booster bolted to a ladder with exposed coax is a different product than one grounded and fused into the coach.
